- **Design system:** LDS 0.9.7 (`v0.9.7-owner.1`, `standalone-0.9.7-r1`, `color-srgb-10`, SHA-256 `d3085cbc…d432d96`). Colours come only from `assets/theme.css` and `js/scales.js`; never approximate a value by eye.
