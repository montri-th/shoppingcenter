- **Design system:** LDS 0.9.7 (`v0.9.7-owner.1`, `standalone-0.9.7-r1`, `color-srgb-10`, SHA-256 `d3085cbc…d432d96`). Colours come only from `assets/theme.css` and `js/scales.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical scales use **all 41 samples** of the exact LDS LUTs (owner decision D8, 2026-10-25 — “scale สีให้ใช้ 41 สี ตาม LDS ทั้งหมด”): `buildRamp()` gives each value the LUT sample of its rank (lowest 0, highest 40, ties share, nothing interpolated) for district fills, province fills and the matrix, and `rampLegendHTML()` draws all 41 samples with value ticks; never a 3/5-class pick, a mixed colour or a per-theme value. Numbers on samples where neither ink reaches 4.5:1 sit on a neutral plate. Categorical groups use `landometer-series-10-v8` with a shape cue per group; no purple or brown, and series 2–3 stay unused because they collide with the orange density scale. No decorative brackets or coloured left rails; keyboard focus always visible.

- **Counting is the product (owner retail-venue definition 2026-10-23):** count only qualified, countable venues; supply excludes future projects and closed-by-evidence; parents never add their components; a qualified component whose parent is not qualified counts as its own venue only when it is a shopping centre or mall (owner decision D6, 2026-10-25: `ORPHAN_COUNTED_GROUPS`, `OWNER_SHOPPING_CENTRES`) and never when it is a possible duplicate; category rules the owner accepted and recorded in the registry (`owner_<rule>_definition_accepted_*`, `owner_confirmed_*`; decision D7) count as qualified with tier `owner`, and the venue panel always says so (“ผ่านนิยามตามกฎที่เจ้าของข้อมูลยอมรับ · <rule>”) — never as source evidence; records still awaiting review and records whose review closed unconfirmed (`reviewed_*_unconfirmed`, state `reviewed`) are quiet grey context labelled by state (zoom ≥ 11, hideable, searchable, outside the tab order) and never enter a number; negative determinations are not shown. Unknown `venue_qualification_status` values must be classified in `qualification_rule()`, and unknown `count_role` values of qualified rows in `COUNTED_ROLES` / `COMPONENT_ROLES` / `HELD_ROLES`, before publishing — the test suite fails until they are. A status that holds the count as a possible duplicate wins over a counted role. A parent the registry closed as context (`parent_context_*`, e.g. ดุสิต เซ็นทรัล พาร์ค) is a negative determination: not shown, not counted; its qualified retail child counts on its own (D6, decision D15).
